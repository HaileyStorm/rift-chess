# Prepared qualification evidence reader

This additive reader separates the accepted prepared-match bundle's historical
creation from its later current99 compiler qualification. It does not replace
`prepared-match/verify.mjs`, redirect any historical consumer, change a pin, grant
an approval or execute a compiler, proof, generated module, subprocess or network
request. The frozen verifier keeps its original live accepted2.0.27 checks.

Initial trust requires an independently reviewed private bootstrap to authenticate
this directory's exact `lineage.json`, its reader/README and declared dependencies
**before importing** the reader. Self-reported hashes cannot establish that trust.
There is no CLI, alternate-path argument, expected-digest override or execution
arm. Root owns native admission, source acceptance and integration.

`capturePreparedQualification()` takes no arguments. It uses bounded regular-file
reads with unredirected real paths and stable descriptor/path observations,
authenticates exact retained bytes, validates record links and settlement, and
returns evidence with `executionAllowed:false`, `adoptionAuthority:false`, zero
fresh proof executions and null kernel/Linux approvals. Its only dynamic import
is the authenticated current99 admission reader; it calls that reader's pure
`validateCurrent99Evidence`, never its Git/Worker/compiler capture path. The
generated prepared JS and helper/producer modules are read as bytes only.

`validatePreparedQualification(lineage, records)` is a synchronous **relational**
validator for independently authenticated parsed inputs. It performs no IO and
cannot establish raw-byte provenance or the full current99 validation on its own.
Its records are `creationManifest`, `parentManifest`, `publication`,
`publicationSteps`, `current99Lineage`, `current99Records`, `sourceReview`,
`emissionTerminal`, `emissionManifest`, `finiteTerminal`, `finiteResult`,
`disposition`, `historicalDifferential`, `historicalEmissionResult`, `reviewText`,
`producerText` and `workloadText`. The lineage fixes the original creation and
later qualification identities; callers cannot choose another evidence set.

Creation authentication covers the five frozen source files, sixteen frozen
evidence files, exact parent manifest and 23-file publication inventory, accepted
review, original no-argument verifier readback, pristine creation compiler commit
and matching canonical-content digest. It relies on that authenticated creation
readback rather than duplicating or weakening the frozen verifier. The parent
manifest is authenticated; current parent meaning and amendment duties remain
separate. Old creator paths are reported as retained, absent or changed descriptive
observations, not checked as the newly adopted current pin.

Later qualification binds the authenticated current99 lineage and eleven records,
exact 99-file inventories across original records, final frozen source/probe/helper/
producer/workload identities, recorded Bun runtime, settled original Popen/handle/
EOF/self-only Job closure and unchanged fences. It checks the exact inverse of
three source-declared constructor-tag rewrites and the retained 61-row/five-control
finite result. Fourteen historical consumer files are authenticated as provenance;
their own revision and execution authority remains intact. Ephemeral historical
claims, checkpoints, Goal/counter paths and external executable locations are
recorded fences, not permanent live reader prerequisites.

The recorded main-thread Bun qualification is not Node Worker 64 MiB acceptance.
Complete erase/frozen equality and ordered carried legal IDs were checked within
one compiler. The older accepted27 comparison covers only recorded labels,
acceptance/carried-count projections and control labels; it does not establish
complete cross-compiler equality. Shared compiler errors remain outside the
finite differential. The reader performs no fresh execution or current live99
inventory check. Consequential current applicability needs fresh source, compiler,
consumer and adoption validation with the existing immutable amendment duties.

Inputs are limited to 16 MiB each and 32 MiB aggregate including readback; this
directory's three sources are capped at 64 KiB. Retained required evidence or
dependency drift/missing bytes rejects capture. Only the explicitly descriptive
old creator-path observations permit absent/changed historical bytes. These limits
and retained qualifications do not prove execution capacity, host/device behavior,
performance, UI activation, Linux/GPU support or adoption.
