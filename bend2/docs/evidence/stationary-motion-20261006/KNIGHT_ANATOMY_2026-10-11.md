# Knight anatomy disposition

The prior knight read as a narrow curved stem beside the atlas pieces. The
rejected muzzle-only trial did not change those proportions enough. This change
reshapes the full neck/head profile: a thicker S-neck, lower jaw, broad cheek,
blunt muzzle and sloped forehead, with shorter ears moved onto the new crown.
The image/mask frame gains top margin and retains its lower pivot margin.

The 332-face topology, 84-face pedestal, material assignments, lighting,
presentation camera and balanced quarter sampling remain intact. Normals,
quarter centers and the common facial outlines are freshly placed on the changed
surfaces. Rendering and atlas picking still use the same projected geometry and
sample occupancy. Finite authoring checks found the four directed components
closed with positive signed volume; this is not a formal geometry or F32 theorem.

Fresh affected Scene and Controller emissions passed whole-book validation with
zero holes. The browser package uses those emissions, retained Menu, current
production ports and the existing bot library. The prepared ground and its
original metadata were reused: the change does not affect ground/underlay code
or its frame inputs. No ground render, repeated formal cone, unit/integration
test or scenario matrix was added.

One real portrait journey passed. After inspecting the actual native-size render,
the controller chose canvas point `(385,468)` on the changed g1 muzzle contour.
The real pointer click selected White Knight g1. Selection and view survived the
held orbit and subsequent detailed refinement. Page/console errors and request
failures were empty. Chrome, loopback server, original process handles, streams
and Job closed normally, with no forced cleanup or uncertain current execution.
