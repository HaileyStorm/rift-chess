## Resolution decision

No higher raster tier is forced by this change. A 2× Canvas backing surface has
four times as many pixels, and sharper sprite alpha sampling needs corresponding
displayed-pixel picking. Current fast sprite textures are 64-square; enlarging
them cannot create new authored detail. The existing Bend quality policy does
not promote at the observed worker rendering times. Changing its thresholds
without supporting measurements would not establish responsiveness. The existing
standard tier, quality fallback, frozen laws and accepted compiler pin remain.

See [current browser acceptance](CURRENT_BROWSER_20261029.md) and
[Canvas limitations](CANVAS_PREVIEW_20261029.md) for the retained functional and
performance scopes. No unit/integration tests or scenario-count gates were added.
