The browser worker includes generated Bend standard-library/runtime code.
The project's constructive proof-helper idioms and small pixel-tree architecture
were informed by the pinned examples, as catalogued in docs/SOURCE_CATALOG.md.
Project-specific modifications and implementations are recorded in this Git
branch. The pinned upstream checkout itself is unchanged.

This experiment includes no downloaded image or sound assets. Sprites and
bitmap fonts are project-authored; sounds are synthesized in Bend and presented
through Web Audio in browsers or the pinned Base Audio effect in native exports.
